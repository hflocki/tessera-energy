# Changelog

## 1.1.4
- On a small card the rings choose a font and icon that fit, and leave out the day where it does not fit.
- Each value in the list is as wide as it is; the names take the rest, smaller on a narrow list.

- Each sensor has its own default entity, and the picture on the Plugins page shows the plugin with values.

## 1.1.2
- On the card, the names under the circles stay inside the card at its edges.

## 1.1.1
- The icon sits inside its circle as one part, and an idle circle is lighter, so the grey icon reads in the light look too.
- Amber and yellow words and icons take the ink on the light look, where they were too faint to read; the dark look keeps its colours.
- Parts not shown are hidden, so nothing lies outside the tile before the first values arrive.

## 1.0.0
